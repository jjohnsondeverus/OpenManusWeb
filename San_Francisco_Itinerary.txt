San Francisco Itinerary

Day 1:
- Golden Gate Bridge: Walk across and explore the surrounding park.
- Crissy Field: Enjoy the beach and views of the harbor.
- Palace of Fine Arts: Visit this Roman-style landmark.
- Alcatraz: Tour the historic prison island.
- Fisherman’s Wharf, Pier 39, and Ghirardelli Square: Explore the waterfront area.
- Mission District: Relax in Dolores Park and explore the local food scene.

Day 2:
- Cable Cars: Ride the iconic San Francisco cable cars.
- Lombard Street: Visit the famous winding street.
- Coit Tower: Enjoy panoramic views of the city.
- Chinatown: Explore the vibrant neighborhood and enjoy dim sum.
- Harbor Tour: Take a cruise to see the city from the water.
- Haight-Ashbury: Discover the historic counterculture neighborhood.
- Castro District: Experience the lively nightlife.

Day 3:
- Walking Tour: Join a guided tour to learn more about the city.
- Ferry Building Marketplace: Enjoy a variety of food options.
- Museums: Visit the Beat Museum, Cable Car Museum, Exploratorium, and De Young Art Museum.
- Golden Gate Park: Explore the vast park and its attractions.
- Nightlife: Relax with a drink in the Mission or Castro districts.

Additional Activities:
- Japantown: Enjoy sushi and Japanese culture.
- Sports Game: Attend a local sports event.
- Wine Country: Visit Napa or Sonoma for wine tasting.
- Muir Woods: See the giant redwoods.
- Berkeley and Oakland: Explore nearby cities.
