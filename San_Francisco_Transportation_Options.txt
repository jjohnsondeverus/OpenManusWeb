San Francisco Transportation Options

Public Transportation:
1. Clipper Card: Use for BART, Muni buses, Metro, and cable cars. Available as a physical card or on your phone.
2. BART: Fast and inexpensive travel from the airport to downtown. Round-trip fare from SFO to downtown is $22.30.
3. Muni: Includes buses, trains, cable cars, and the F-line streetcar. Use MuniMobile app or Clipper Card for payment.
4. Cable Cars and Street Cars: Historic transit options. Tickets available in advance or via smartphone.
5. PresidiGo Shuttle: Free transportation around the Presidio area.

Ride-Sharing and Taxis:
- Ride-Sharing Apps: Services like Lyft and Uber available throughout the city.
- Taxis: Can be hailed directly or via smartphone apps.

Biking and Scooters:
- Bike Share: Bay Wheels bike share program for short trips.
- Scooters: Available through apps like Lime and Spin.

Ferries:
- Ferry Services: Travel to nearby locations like Sausalito and Tiburon. Payment options include Clipper Card and mobile apps.

Renting a Car:
- Car Rentals: Available for exploring areas beyond the city. Be cautious about parking and theft.
