Packing List for Mobile Botanical Garden Visit:

1. Camera: To capture the beautiful scenery and memories.
2. Water Bottle: Stay hydrated during your visit.
3. Snacks: Light snacks to keep your energy up.
4. Comfortable Footwear: Suitable for walking around the garden.
5. Hat and Sunglasses: Protection from the sun.
6. Sunscreen: To protect your skin from UV rays.
7. Notebook or Sketchpad: If you enjoy drawing or taking notes.
8. Backpack or Tote Bag: To carry your items comfortably.
9. Umbrella or Raincoat: In case of unexpected rain.
10. Map of the Garden: If available, to help navigate.
